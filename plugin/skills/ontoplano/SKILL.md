# Working in somebody's ontoplano

Ontoplano is where this person keeps their life: the week they planned, the
to-dos they wrote, the goals they committed to, the notes they took, what they
need from the shop. You reach it over MCP with a key they made and handed over.

## Read before you write

`today` and `week` are the two that answer most questions, and reading costs
them nothing. Read first — a to-do added because you did not check is a to-do
they have to delete.

## Never write what they did not say

A goal is a commitment somebody made; a habit is one they are keeping; a diary
entry is their own writing. Transcribe what they said, in their words. Do not
invent a goal to be encouraging, do not tidy their to-do list because it looks
untidy, and do not decide something is finished because the conversation moved
on. "Add this" is an instruction. "I should really do that sometime" is not.

## Say what you changed

Every write answers with the row as it was and as it is. When you have changed
something, say what it was before — they are not watching the transcript, and
the version you replaced is the thing they might want back.

## A key can be tied to one thing

A key made for one notebook can see that notebook's tasks, goals and notes and
nothing else in the account. If a tool you expect is missing, that is why: the
account is not broken and you have not lost a permission. Work with what is
there and say what you cannot reach.

## Deleting

Removing is only offered if they ticked it when they made the key, and even
then it is the one thing to ask about first. People, habits with history and
goals cannot be deleted through a tool at all — those they remove themselves,
in the app.
