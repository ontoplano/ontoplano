The first open test of Ontoplano on Android.

The whole app: your week as blocks of time, goals with measured progress,
notebooks, habits and workouts, finances, inventory, recipes and the shopping
list — and reminders that arrive on the phone.

Sign in to an instance you host, to app.ontoplano.com, or keep everything on
the phone with no account and no network at all.

Tell me what breaks: github.com/ontoplano/ontoplano/issues
