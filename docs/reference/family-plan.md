# The family plan

One subscription covering up to five accounts. Everybody keeps their own week,
their own notes, their own everything — nothing is shared but the invoice. A
seat is access, never the ability to spend: the billing pages belong to
whoever holds the card.

## Setting it up

1. Buy the family plan — the option is on the register page, and on
   **Settings → Billing** if you started solo.
2. Open **Settings → Family** and type an email address.

That is the whole procedure, and it does not matter whether that address has
an account yet:

- **Already has one** — they are on your plan the moment you press the button.
- **Does not** — an account is made for them on the spot, and they get an
  email saying you are paying for it. The link in it opens their account,
  already signed in, at the first-run setup. They pick a password whenever
  they like with "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.

## Leaving, and ending

Taking somebody off the plan (**Settings → Family**, the × beside their name)
removes the seat and nothing else: their account and everything in it stays,
and it is theirs to subscribe for or export. If the plan itself lapses, every
seat lapses with it the same way — everything kept, everything exportable,
nothing else working until somebody pays.
